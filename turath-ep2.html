The Sweet Secret That Built an Empire

Episode 2 · Saadian Sugar | Documentary series

Imagine Morocco as one of the biggest sugar exporters in the world. Great empires were built on it, and palaces rose from it. Some of them still stand today. But how did it happen?

Five hundred years ago, Morocco faced an enemy it had never seen before. Foreign fleets were seizing the coast, one city after another, while the country was divided and weak. In the south, tribes that had rarely stood together made a decision that would change everything, and from it a new dynasty was born, the Saadians. Their story is unusual, because power came looking for them rather than the other way around.

But power needs an army, and an army needs money. So where does a young state find a fortune? In this episode, Zakaria follows the answer to the countryside near Essaouira, a place that is not far from the crowds, yet almost no one knows it. Hidden there is the trace of a once mighty industry that reached the farthest lands on earth.

Walk through the ruins of giant factories. Follow canals that carried water across dozens of kilometers. Discover how raw cane became “sugar as pure as milk” that European ships crossed the sea to buy. And hear about the true price of that sweetness, which was never measured in gold alone.

Along the way, Zakaria stands where history was left to rest, and finds out just how alone these treasures really are.

This is not only a story about money and power. It is a tribute to the people who made the sugar, and to what they left behind.
